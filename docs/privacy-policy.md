# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 4, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Dikalastory ("Service", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our AI-assisted cinematic story and production planning application.

## 1. Information we process

Depending on features you use, Service may process:

- project title, description, genre, language, tone, duration, and target platform;
- story text, research inputs, references, citations, and instructions;
- character, location, object, scene, shot, prompt, continuity, and asset information;
- generated AI output, pipeline status, logs, telemetry, model usage, and quota information;
- image, audio, video URLs, and asset references supplied by you;
- provider credentials or API keys that you voluntarily configure;
- Google account identity and OAuth access token needed for optional Drive import/export.

Service does not intentionally collect advertising IDs, browser fingerprints, or precise device location based on current implementation.

## 2. How we use information

We use information to:

- provide, secure, maintain, and troubleshoot Service;
- save and retrieve projects;
- process prompts through Google Gemini or another provider selected in settings;
- generate story, scene, shot, prompt, continuity, and production-planning results;
- import files from and export files to Google Drive when you request it;
- monitor usage, quotas, errors, performance, and abuse;
- respond to support requests and comply with law.

We do not sell personal information.

## 3. Google services

Optional Drive features use Firebase Authentication and Google OAuth. Service requests the Google Drive `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file` scope to create, access, or manage files used with Service. Google handles authentication and its services under Google’s own policies.

You can revoke access from your Google Account security settings. Revocation can stop Drive features from working; it does not automatically delete copies already stored in Service or Drive.

## 4. AI and other providers

Content sent for generation may be processed by Google Gemini or other AI providers configured by you, including OpenAI-compatible providers. Provider retention, training, security, and regional processing practices may differ. Review each provider’s privacy policy and settings before sending sensitive content.

## 5. Storage and retention

Projects, generated results, operational logs, and telemetry may be stored in Firestore or local/server storage used by deployment. We retain information while needed to provide Service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or maintain security. Retention periods depend on data type and operational needs.

Delete projects or request deletion through the Service owner’s published contact channel. Backups, logs, or legal records may remain for a limited period where required or reasonably necessary.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. No online system is completely secure. Do not submit passwords, payment card data, government ID numbers, health data, or other sensitive information unless Service explicitly requires it.

Protect your Google account and any provider credentials. If you believe credentials or data were exposed, rotate credentials immediately and contact the Service owner.

## 7. Sharing

We may share information:

- with infrastructure, hosting, database, authentication, AI, and storage providers needed to operate requested features;
- when you direct Service to export or share content;
- to comply with law, legal process, or valid governmental request;
- to protect rights, safety, security, and Service integrity;
- in connection with a merger, acquisition, or transfer of Service, subject to applicable law.

## 8. Children

Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe such information was submitted, contact the Service owner for removal.

## 9. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or information about processing of your personal information. You may stop using optional Drive features and revoke Google access. Send requests through the Service owner’s published contact channel; identity verification may be required.

## 10. International processing

Service providers may process information in countries different from your country of residence. Applicable safeguards depend on provider and deployment configuration.

## 11. Policy changes

We may update this Policy when Service, providers, or legal requirements change. We will publish the updated version with a new date. Continued use after an update means acceptance where legally permitted.

## 12. Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or complaints, contact the Service owner through the contact channel published with the deployed application.
